2. **Create New Application**:
   - Click **Create App** (top right corner).
   - Enter **App Name**: `Second Brain AI System`.
   - Default language: `English (US)` $\rightarrow$ App Type: `App` $\rightarrow$ Free / Paid: `Free`.

3. **Upload the `.aab` Bundle**:
   - In the left sidebar, navigate to **Production** (or **Testing** $\rightarrow$ **Internal testing**).
   - Click **Create new release**.
   - Drag & drop your generated `app-release-bundle.aab` file.
   - Enter Release Name: `1.0.0` and click **Save**.

4. **Complete Store Listing**:
   - Navigate to **Store presence** $\rightarrow$ **Main store listing**.
   - Paste the Short Description & Full Description provided above.
   - Upload **App Icon** (512x512 PNG from `assets/nexus_yellow_bot.png`).
   - Upload at least 2 Phone Screenshots.

5. **Complete Content Rating & App Content Questionnaires**:
   - Navigate to **App content** in the sidebar.
   - Complete **Privacy Policy**, **Content Rating Questionnaire** (General / Universal), and **Target Audience**.

6. **Rollout to Production**:
   - Click **Review release** $\rightarrow$ **Start rollout to Production**!
   - Google will review your submission (usually approved in 24–48 hours).
